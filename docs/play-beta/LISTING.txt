Title
Wanderlust

Short description
Record your journeys, colour your map and save memories along the way.

Full description
Explore at your own pace. Wanderlust brings your walks, rides and other journeys together on one personal map. See where you have been and keep the small things worth remembering.

• Tap Start recording and put your phone away. A visible notification accompanies screen-off recording.
• Download nearby OpenStreetMap maps and use them offline.
• See recorded routes on your map, with street matching where map data and GPS quality allow it.
• Save notes and photos with optional map pins. Browse memory cards, swipe between memories and zoom into photos.
• Review saved journeys and GPS recording summaries.
• Check your heading with the small live compass on supported devices.
• Export GPX tracks and make or restore full backups of journeys, memories, maps and settings.

There are no accounts, advertisements, scores or leaderboards. Recorded routes and memories stay in the app's local storage. Map downloads send the requested geographic area to the Overpass map service; this can reveal your location. Automatic map downloads can be disabled in Settings. The app includes no analytics.

This is an early beta. GPS accuracy and screen-off recording behaviour vary by phone. Street matching can leave gaps or make mistakes. Android battery restrictions or force-stopping the app can interrupt recording. Keep a full backup before changing phones or switching app installations.
