Explore at your own pace. Wanderlust colours the streets you walk or cycle and deepens their colour when you return. There are no scores to chase, accounts to create or advertisements.

• Download nearby OpenStreetMap streets for offline walks, from 1 to 25 km per side.
• See explored streets colour in as you walk, with warmer colours for repeat visits.
• Keep notes and photos of small things worth remembering, with optional map pins.
• Find favourite walks in your history. Every recorded walk counts towards your map.
• Record different kinds of journeys with automatic GPS sampling or your preferred interval.
• Choose whether to show street names, timing and GPS details.
• Export GPX when you want it. Make and restore full backups, including memories and maps.

Location is used with your permission. A visible notification accompanies screen-off recording. Routes and memories are stored locally. Downloading a map sends the requested area to the Overpass map service. The app includes no analytics or Google Play Services dependency.

Wanderlust is early software. GPS accuracy and battery behaviour vary by device; street matching may leave gaps. Force-stopping the app or the operating system ending its process can interrupt recording. Previously committed readings are recovered when you reopen it.
