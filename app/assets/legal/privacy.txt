Wanderlust privacy policy
Updated 30 September 2026

Wanderlust is developed by Uwe Trostheide. It is an offline-first walking map.
There are no accounts, advertising or analytics in the app.

Location and walks
With your permission, Wanderlust uses your location to show where you are,
find nearby maps, pin memories and record walks. Starting a walk enables a
location foreground service and a notification so recording can continue with
the screen off. Stop ends recording. There is no automatic tracking on boot.
GPS readings and explored-street counts are stored in the app's private storage.
The app does not upload your recorded routes to the developer.
Android and your device's location provider have their own privacy settings.

Map downloads
When you choose to download a map, the app sends the requested geographic
bounding box to https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter over HTTPS. This area
can reveal your approximate location. The service also receives your IP address
and an app-identifying user agent as part of the request. Maps are not downloaded
automatically. The Overpass operator controls its own server logs and retention;
see https://overpass-api.de/ for operator information. Downloaded OpenStreetMap
streets and scenery are saved locally. No account is required.

Memories and photos
Notes, optional coordinates and attached photos stay in the app's private
storage. Taking or choosing a photo uses your camera or Android photo picker.
We do not scan your photo library. A source photo may remain in the camera or
photo app and follow that app's backup policy. Photo metadata may be retained;
consider this before sharing a backup. Drafts are stored locally too.

Exports, backups and deletion
GPX exports and full backups are created only when you request them. Full backups
can include GPS routes, memories, photos, offline maps and settings. Export files
are not encrypted by Wanderlust. Your chosen document provider may upload files
to a cloud service; choose a local destination if you want them to stay offline.
Wanderlust excludes its private data from Android automatic cloud backup and
device transfer. Use the app's manual full backup before changing phones.
Local data remains until you delete it or clear the app's storage/uninstall it.
Deleting a walk rebuilds its heatmap contribution; favouriting does not change it.
Deleting local data does not delete copies you have exported or original photos
managed by another app. Previously created device/cloud backups are controlled
by your operating system or backup provider.

External links and contact
Opening project or map-provider pages is subject to those sites' policies.
For support or privacy questions, contact the maintainer through
https://github.com/utrost/Wanderlust/issues . Issues are public: do not post
private routes, photos or other sensitive information there.

Changes
Updates to this policy are included with app releases and in the source
repository. Source code: https://github.com/utrost/Wanderlust
